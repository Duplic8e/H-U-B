words.json in this folder is a derived dataset.

1. FreeDict French-English dictionary (fra-eng)
   https://github.com/freedict/fd-dictionaries/tree/master/fra-eng
   Licence: GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later version.
   Used for: the French headwords, English translations, part of speech, gender and pronunciation.
   Changes: reduced to up to three translations per word, entries with the same spelling merged,
   and the glosses of about 85 very common function words rewritten by hand.

2. FrequencyWords, French (fr_50k, OpenSubtitles 2018) by Hermit Dave
   https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (content).
   Used for: the order of the words, most common first. Words not in that list were left out.
